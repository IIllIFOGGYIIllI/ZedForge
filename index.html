SPAWN CONFIGURATION / ATTACHMENTS & CARGO

Spawnable Studio.

Configure how equipped items and container contents spawn. Changes are applied to your uploaded cfgspawnabletypes.xml without recreating unrelated XML elements.

How it works: Group chance determines whether the attachments/cargo group is evaluated; individual item chance controls an item within the group. Damage 0.0–0.0 means pristine. This editor does not change population values in types.xml.
